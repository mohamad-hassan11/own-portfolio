## Specialising: mobile, data and AI

In the later stage of the programme I specialised further: an Android Mobile Development semester with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, a Big Data minor building a production-oriented analytics platform with an external company (Arkive) using Flask, Dash and Docker, and a Software Engineering specialisation semester covering design patterns, testing and containerised CI/CD.

## Graduation: secure RAG integration

My graduation project at DEVENTit investigated how to securely integrate a large language model into Atlantis, a multi-tenant collection management system, without bypassing its strict role-based access control. I designed an indexing pipeline and a hybrid retrieval pipeline combining dense vector embeddings with BM25 keyword search, delivering a proof of concept where the RAG service handles LLM reasoning while Atlantis retains full access control authority.
